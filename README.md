# Two-Sentence Horror

```
I dialed my missing son's phone just to hear his voicemail greeting once more in the quiet house. The ringtone started chiming softly from inside the drywall behind my headboard.
```

### About the Story
A two-sentence psychological horror story where a grieving parent's tender moment of longing turns into suffocating dread upon discovering their missing child is concealed inside the bedroom wall.
